Ask Abby — 0330 533 0665
How are you different from other AI agencies?

Most agencies are "AI-first" — they start with a platform and find a place to shoehorn it. We are process-first. We assess your business to find the revenue leaks and bottlenecks, then engineer the AI to fix them. We're obsessive about increasing your ROI. If you're happy, we're happy.

How do I know if we're ready?

If you have repetitive manual work, fragmented data, or revenue bleeding from slow response times, you're already paying the price for AI. We'll tell you exactly which processes are AI-ready and which aren't.

What happens after you deliver?

We don't drop a product and run. We monitor the feedback loops and refine the model until it's a self-sustaining part of your operations. We don't leave until you're running better than before.

How much of my team's time will this take?

We work on the principle of high-leverage input. We need your context, not your time. We handle the heavy lifting of development and integration so you can focus on the core business. You provide the goals; we provide the solution.

How long until we see results?

Weeks, not months. Our model is to deploy a high-impact pilot immediately. We believe in the prove-then-scale approach: we show you the ROI on one specific workflow first, then scale from there.

I don't have big data or a big tech team. Can we still do this?

That's the most common starting point. We design solutions that work with the data you have today, not the perfect data you wish you had. We bridge the technical gap so you don't need to hire a fleet of developers.

We tried AI before and it failed. Why is this different?

Most AI projects fail because they target the wrong problem or ignore the human side. We focus on high-value, high-certainty workflows, and we stay attached to you so you actually see the results.
